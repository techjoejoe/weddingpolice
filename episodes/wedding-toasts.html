Show Notes

In this episode of The Wedding Police, Penny and Jim put AI to the test by attempting to generate the perfect wedding toast—and quickly discover it’s not as simple as it sounds. With their usual tongue-in-cheek “law enforcement” approach, they critique the AI’s results while highlighting common toast mistakes, from awkward phrasing to saying way too much. Along the way, they lay down the law on what to avoid, what actually works, and why the best speeches are always short, sincere, and to the point.
