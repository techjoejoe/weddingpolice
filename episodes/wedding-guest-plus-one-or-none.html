PLUS-ONE PANDEMONIUM: Because Apparently Everyone Needs a Date

This week on The Wedding Police, Penny and Jim tackle the wedding minefield known as the plus-one. Is your third Tinder date really worthy of a $200 dinner? Does your dog count as a guest? And why does your friend who has been single for six years suddenly think the wedding is her personal dating event?

Penny and Jim dive into the rules, the ridiculousness, and the inevitable hurt feelings. Because when it comes to weddings, apparently “You’re invited” doesn’t necessarily mean “Bring whoever you want.” 😂💍
