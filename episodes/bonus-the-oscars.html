Show Notes


In this BONUS episode of The Wedding Police, Penny and Jim dive into the Oscars with equal parts style critique and chaos. Penny delivers sharp takes on red carpet trends while Jim provides deeply unserious commentary.

They bounce between fashion highs, pop culture moments, and slightly unhinged observations about celebrity behavior, turning the biggest night in Hollywood into a full-on comedy recap. By the end, they’ve learned nothing, judged everything, and somehow made the Oscars feel like a group chat you wish you were in.
