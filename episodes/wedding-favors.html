Episode 11 of Wedding Police finds Penny and Jim diving into the often-debated world of wedding favors. From thoughtful keepsakes guests actually love to the all-too-common trinkets left behind at the end of the night, they weigh the real value of these mini gifts. With their signature humor and honest takes, Penny and Jim explore whether wedding favors are a meaningful gesture—or an unnecessary expense couples can skip.
