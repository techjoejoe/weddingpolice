Show Notes

In this episode of The Wedding Police, Penny and Jim tackle one of the most debated reception topics: games at your wedding. Are they a fun icebreaker or a total vibe-killer? The duo shares real stories, dos and don’ts, and their favorite ways to get guests involved without forcing awkward moments. Plus, they introduce a new sponsor, www.myweddingtrivia.com, a simple and customizable way to bring personalized trivia to your big day. Whether you’re team “dance floor only” or love a little friendly competition, this episode has something for every couple planning their celebration.
