Show Notes

On this week's podcast, the confetti has settled, the cake has been sliced, and we're still reviewing the game film.

Penny and Jim return for Part Two of the completely unofficial, wildly over-speculated, post-game analysis of the wedding that had the internet calling time-out: Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce's big day. Was the seating chart a strategic masterpiece? Was there really a buffet? Did the first dance deserve a standing ovation? And which guest nailed the Karaoke?

Grab your leftover wedding cake, tighten your tuxedo, and prepare for another round of hot takes, questionable opinions, and far more analysis than any wedding probably needs. The Wedding Singerhas left the building, but the Wedding Police are just getting started.
