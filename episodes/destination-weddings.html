Show Notes

This week on The Wedding Police, Penny and Jim investigate the wild world of destination weddings. Are they romantic celebrations in paradise, or cleverly disguised group vacations that require guests to spend their rent money on airfare? From passport panic and all-inclusive resorts to beach ceremonies and family drama, Penny and Jilt examine the evidence, issue a few citations, and determine whether destination weddings are guilty of wedding crimes—or deserve a full acquittal.
