Show Notes

In this episode of The Wedding Police Podcast, Penny and Jim are joined by special guest and sponsor Ryan Gorey from RGML Entertainment to talk all things wedding DJs. Ryan shares hilarious and memorable stories from the DJ booth, including unexpected song requests, dance floor disasters, and the moments that turned receptions into unforgettable parties. The crew also discusses what couples should look for in a wedding DJ, how to avoid common music mistakes, and why the right entertainment can make or break your wedding day. Tune in for laughs, expert advice, and plenty of wedding reception drama! 💃🕺🎶
