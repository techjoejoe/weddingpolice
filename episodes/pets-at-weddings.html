Show Notes

This week, Penny and Jim investigate the furry frontier of modern weddings in this hilarious episode of The Wedding Police. Should your dog be the ring bearer? Should a Falcon attend cocktail hour? And who’s responsible when Grandma gets tackled by an overexcited golden retriever? Special guest Mary — professional dog handler and self-proclaimed “canine canoodler” — shares her advice, and how to tell if your chihuahua is emotionally prepared for a first dance. Chaos, barking, and suspiciously wet noses guaranteed.
