Show Notes

🚨🎂 Episode 18 of The Wedding Police Podcast is serving up a sweet dose of wedding cake controversy! Penny and Jim take a bite out of the wild world of wedding cake toppers—from elegant and timeless to downright questionable. Is that bride dragging the groom by a fishing pole still funny? Should superhero figurines be standing on your five-tier masterpiece? And how many hobbies can you cram onto one tiny cake topper before it becomes a crime against confectionery? Join the Wedding Police as they investigate the tackiest toppers, celebrate the creative ones, and determine which cake decorations deserve a citation. Grab a fork—this episode is layered with laughs!
