1996 vs. 2026: Wedding Edition! 💍📱😂

This week on the Wedding Police Podcast, Penny & Jim take a hilarious trip down memory lane to compare weddings in 1996 vs. 2026!

In 1996, you had a disposable camera, a DJ, a three-tier cake, and maybe a fax machine involved. In 2026, you’ve got drones, content creators, QR codes, choreographed entrances, personalized cocktails, and enough wedding apps to require their own IT department! 😂

From “Please RSVP by mail” to “Scan this QR code and tell us your meal, song request, hotel preference, and emotional support animal”—weddings have definitely changed!

Tune in as Penny & Jim decide whether weddings have gotten better… or just a LOT more complicated! 🚨👰🤵
