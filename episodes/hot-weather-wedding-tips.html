Hot Hot Hot: Keeping Guests Cool at a Summer Wedding

A Wedding Police case file with Penny & Jim.

▶ Play this episode
🎵 Listen on Spotify 🎙 Apple Podcasts

Show Notes

This week on Wedding Police, Penny and Jim are on heatwave patrol! If your wedding day forecast is "hot enough to fry an egg on the dance floor," don't panic. They cover smart (and funny) ways to keep your guests cool, from handheld and misting fans to hydration stations, cool desserts, and creating comfortable indoor and outdoor spaces. They also explain why giant industrial fans and carefully arranged centerpieces are not the best of friends. Tune in for practical tips, plenty of laughs, and simple ways to make sure the only thing raising the temperature on your wedding day is the romance.
