This week on The Wedding Police, Penny and Jim welcome Janet, who believes the stars may have more to say about your wedding than your mother, your maid of honor, and your bank account combined! Can your zodiac sign predict your bridal style, your future in-laws, or a seating-chart catastrophe? Is it written in the stars—or are we just blaming Saturn for bad decisions? Janet is decoding all the signs and one thing’s for certain: even if all the planets align, somebody still has to pay the caterer! 🌙😂
