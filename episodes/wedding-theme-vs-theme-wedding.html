Show Notes

This week on Wedding Police, Penny and Jim investigate the wild world of wedding themes. From fairytale castles and rustic barns to masquerade balls and questionable tropical luaus, they examine what makes a themed wedding memorable—and what might earn a citation from the Wedding Police. Join them as they separate charming creativity from full-blown theme overload, with plenty of laughs along the way.
