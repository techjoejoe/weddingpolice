Episode Recap: Wedding Police – Chaos Case File

In this week’s episode of Wedding Police, the hosts investigate a wedding that somehow transformed from a formal celebration into a full-blown social disaster. What should have been a smooth, joyful day instead turned into a masterclass in how not to behave in public.

Penny & Jim decide lean in to who are the usual suspects, who needs to be held without bail, and how the couple can "RECLAIM THEIR TIME"

By the end, the hosts agree on one key takeaway: weddings don’t fall apart because of the couple—they fall apart because someone in the crowd decides the day is secretly about them.
