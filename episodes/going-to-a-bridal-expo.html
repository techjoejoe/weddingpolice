Show Notes

This week on The Wedding Police, Penny and Jim hit the aisles of the bustling bridal expo with special guest Bill Brennan, CEO of Bridal Expo Chicago/Milwaukee. From the hottest wedding trends and must-see vendors to insider tips on making the most of a bridal show (and get the best cake samples) Bill shares decades of industry expertise. Whether you're newly engaged or just love all things weddings, this episode is packed with advice, laughs, and plenty of behind-the-scenes stories from the world of bridal expos including some over the top Bridal Expo Chaos that needs to be addressed.
